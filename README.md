Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Laugh** (`u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3`).

The program (`main.py`) writes one funny line to `/output/entry.txt`. The line is the whole entry — capped at 200 characters, judged two at a time by the house judge.

> My bank flagged a $3 coffee as fraud. Even my bank doesn't believe I leave the house.

85 characters. Original work, written from the brief.
